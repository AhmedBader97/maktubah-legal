Delete your Maktubah account

Applies to the Maktubah Android app (com.maktubah.app)

In the app (instant)

Open Profile → Settings → Your data → Delete my account and confirm twice. Your profile, photos, preferences, matches, messages, voice notes and verification selfie are deleted permanently, immediately.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, email maktubah.app@gmail.com from any address with the phone number you signed up with. We send a code to that number to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account: your profile and photos, preferences and visibility settings, invitations, requests, matches and conversations (messages, photos and voice notes you sent), reports you filed, your verification selfie, saved and passed profiles, and your sign-in record.

What we keep

A minimal record that lets us keep banned accounts from returning (a hashed identifier only, never your profile), and purchase records for the period the law requires. Nothing that identifies you remains visible to other members.

Deleting only some data

You can remove individual photos, messages you sent, saved profiles and preferences at any time inside the app without deleting your account.
