Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

1. The short version

No man ever sees a woman’s photos or identity unless she chooses him. We collect only what the service needs, we do not sell your data, and you can delete everything from Settings at any time.

2. What we collect

Account: phone number, gender, date of birth. Profile: the details, photos and prompts you add, plus your visibility choices. Usage: invitations, requests, matches, messages, reports, blocks, and anonymised counts such as how often your profile was shown. Technical: device type, app version and coarse diagnostics. Location is the city you type — we never read GPS.

3. What we use it for

To run the matching mechanism you signed up for; to enforce women’s visibility choices; to rank and filter decks; to process payments (male side only); to keep users safe through verification, moderation and abuse prevention; and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data, and we do not show third-party advertising.

4. Who can see what

Women: your biodata (minus fields you hide) is readable by men, with your first name, only while discovery is on; your photos are released only to a man you invite or accept, and are withdrawn again if the connection closes. Men: your profile and photos are visible to women in their decks. Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Our staff access personal data only when needed for safety, support or legal compliance.

5. Processors we rely on

We use a small number of processors to run the service: cloud database and file storage, SMS delivery for sign-in codes, image checks for verification and prohibited content, payment processing via the app store, and crash/analytics tooling configured without advertising identifiers. Each processes data only on our instructions.

6. How long we keep things

Your data lives while your account does. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, preferences, matches and messages. We keep a minimal ban record (hashed identifiers) to stop banned users returning, and retain records the law requires (for example purchase records) for their statutory period.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or erase your data. Deletion is self-service in Settings → Your data; for export and the other rights, use Contact support in Settings. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO). We answer rights requests within one month.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Photos live in private storage served through short-lived signed links. Access rules are enforced in the database itself, so even a modified app cannot read what it should not. Sensitive screens block screenshots and screen recording where the operating system allows it (Android and iPhone); on iPhone a screenshot attempt is also recorded and the other person is informed. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Children

The service is for adults only. We do not knowingly hold data on anyone under 18; date of birth is checked at signup and accounts found to be underage are deleted.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy materially we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. Questions and rights requests: use the support option in Settings. English is the governing language of this policy.
